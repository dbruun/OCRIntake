Extraction settings

Product intake

Create reusable profiles for different product types. Specify what to look for on the image—not what to guess.

Saving creates a new immutable profile version. Existing intakes keep their original fields and instructions. In live mode, the backend creates the selected version's Microsoft Content Understanding analyzer on its first upload. Sample mode returns synthetic values and does not inspect images. Compliance rules are configured separately.

Profile definition

Define 1–30 fields. A stable key identifies the field in saved records and compliance rules; changing it creates a different field. All extracted values are text, preserving printed units and wording. Confidence may be unavailable.
